## Layer Model

- 1. Access and recovery: The production page can be reached, authenticated, monitored, and recovered without guessing.
- 2. Purpose and orientation: The first screen makes the page purpose, owner, environment, decisions, and failure states clear.
- 3. Live data contract: The page consumes a standard live payload with freshness, source, ownership, incidents, and links.
- 4. Operational summary: The first screen answers whether the system is okay, what changed, and what needs attention.
- 5. Drilldowns: No important card is a dead end; every summary signal can be investigated.
- 6. History and trends: The page can explain whether things are improving, slowing, or silently degrading.
- 7. Governed actions: The page can safely operate the system through permissioned, audited, reversible actions.
- 8. Incidents and alerts: The page tells the operator what broke, why it matters, and what to do next.
- 9. Business workflow: The page is domain-specific enough to run the actual project or system area.
- 10. Rollup and certification: The page feeds the executive rollup and cannot claim fully operational without proof.
